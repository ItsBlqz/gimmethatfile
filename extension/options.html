Which downloads to send to the app

Good to know

If the app isn't running, or capture is off in its settings, the browser downloads the file as usual. Private windows are never captured. Right-click a link and choose “Download with Gimme That File” to send it by hand.

Cookies for the file's site are sent to the app so downloads that need a login work, and are kept in the app's local download list so they can be resumed.
